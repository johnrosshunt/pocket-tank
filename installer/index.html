Install Pocket Tank

A tiny fish tank where a small AI decides what the fish do, all on the board. Install it from this page. Update it the same way.

Install

  1. Plug the board into your computer. Use a USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.
  2. Click Install. Pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit from the list. On Linux it may say ttyACM0.
  3. Wait a minute or two. When it says Installation complete, the board restarts by itself.
    • New board: you get a fresh tank with two baby fish.
    • Already have a tank: it comes back just as you left it.

Updating? Use the same button. It never erases your tank: your fish, their names, badges, sand dollars and decorations all stay.

The pop-up says Install even when you're updating. That's normal. If the screen is dark, the tank is asleep: press PWR firmly to wake it first, or it won't show up in the list.

Start over

To reset your tank, you don't need this page. Hold BOOT, tap the screen, and answer YES. You get two new baby fish.

Board not starting, or want a clean wipe? This button erases everything, then installs fresh. Your tank will be gone.

In the pop-up, tick Erase device. If you leave it unticked, it just installs like the red button.

Which board?

Supported

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.8, version V1 or V2. About $35 with the screen and a battery.

Other boards won't work yet. The installer may still run on them, but the screen will stay blank.

Having trouble?

My board isn't in the list

If the screen is dark, the tank is asleep. Press PWR firmly to wake it, then try again.

Still missing? Hold BOOT while you plug in the cable. That always makes it show up.

Also try another cable, and plug straight into the computer, not a hub.

The install fails or stops partway

Close anything else using the board, like a serial monitor or this page in another tab. Unplug, plug back in, and click Install again.

Still failing? Hold BOOT while you plug in, then try again. Your tank is safe either way.

Linux: "permission denied"

Run sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER, then log out and back in. (On some systems the group is uucp.)

Windows: it shows up but won't connect

Windows 10 and 11 already have the driver. If Device Manager shows a warning on the board, install Espressif's USB driver or try another USB port.

Back up my tank first (optional, uses a terminal)

Updates keep your tank, but if you'd like a copy: install esptool (pip install esptool) and wake the tank. To save it: esptool.py --chip esp32s3 read_flash 0x9000 0x6000 my-tank.bin. To put it back: esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x9000 my-tank.bin

What gets installed?

The app, the 7.5 MB AI model, and two small startup files. Your tank's save sits in a separate spot the installer never touches. Only the erase button clears it.

Everything runs on the board. Nothing is sent anywhere.

After it starts

A new tank opens with a quick setup: place the bubbles, then name your two fish and pick their colors. Then:

  • Feed: tap the water.
  • Say hi: hold a finger on the glass and they'll come over.
  • Clean: drag across the glass to wipe off algae.
  • Trim: swipe sideways through the grass.
  • Light: double-tap the glass.
  • Sleep: press PWR. Press it firmly again to wake.
  • Reset: hold BOOT and tap the glass.
